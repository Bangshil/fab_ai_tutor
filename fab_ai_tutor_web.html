Course AI Ideas Applied

Credit Assignment: identify which passages deserve credit for helping a student understand a question.

Information Processing: reduce the burden of reading every page by surfacing a small evidence set.

Search: treat the query as a goal and use a heuristic to find promising course chunks.

Representation: convert PDFs into structured chunks with source, page, and text.

KRR: use course passages as a knowledge base for evidence-based answer scaffolds.

Interaction: support study through search, answer draft, quiz, and prompt modes.

Aggregation: gather competing candidate passages into a shared evidence board.
